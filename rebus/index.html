Rebus

Every word of the sentence is drawn as a picture puzzle. Read it, then build the missing word.


How to play

Sound out the pictures. They spell a sentence.

  1. Each group of pictures and letters is one word. Say the pieces aloud and run them together.
  2. Pictures say what they show. Letters say their name, or their sound when they spell the word's edge.
  3. Each clue leaves a word blank. Work out what it must be, then build it from the tiles below. Tap the blank you want to fill — every clue shares the same tiles, so working out which tiles belong where is part of the puzzle.
  4. Press Enter to check. A tile may be used more than once.
